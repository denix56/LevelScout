ranks zones, orders quests into a route and tells you when to move on.

1. LevelScout's code
   Every file outside LevelScout/data/. GPL-3.0.

2. The game data
   LevelScout/data/*.lua holds data from ForeverDB (a, which includes data
   from CMaNGOS classic-db, c), generated by tools/import/import.lua from
   ForeverDB alone. QuestieDB's data (b) is not shipped: LevelScout reads it
   at runtime from the QuestieDB addon the player installs. Every data file
   starts with the line
     -- Data: ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net), build <client build>. ...
   Keep that line, and the other notices, in any file you take.

   a. ForeverDB, by ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)
      Copyright (C) 2026 ForeverDB.net. GPL-3.0 with the attribution term
      quoted below. LevelScout takes from it: objective counts; quest,
      objective and turn-in locations observed in Forever; drop chances
      measured in Forever; quest XP captures and its XP curve; pace (seconds
      per kill, item and object); zone levels; travel data (flight paths and
      times, hubs, walking distances); the list of classic quests that exist
      in the Forever client.

      ForeverDB's licence (its LICENSE.txt) sets this additional term:

        Additional term (GPL-3.0 section 7(b)): attribution
        This term applies to ForeverDB.net's own material in this addon: its
        code, and the data ForeverDB.net built and ForeverDB players recorded
        in WoW Forever (see NOTICE.txt for which data that is). If you copy,
        modify or convey this addon, any of its files, or data taken from
        them, in any form (another addon, a website, an app, a bot, a
        spreadsheet), you must:

          1. keep the line that starts "-- Data: ForeverDB.net
             (https://foreverdb.net)" and the other copyright and licence
             notices in every file you take, and
          2. show this attribution, with the link, in the Appropriate Legal
             Notices of the work that contains it (its credits, its about
             text, or wherever it lists its licences and sources):

                 Data from ForeverDB.net (https://foreverdb.net)

      ("this addon" and "NOTICE.txt" there mean ForeverDB and its own
      NOTICE.txt.) ForeverDB's NOTICE.txt labels its data by where it comes
      from: what ForeverDB players' addons recorded in WoW Forever and
      ForeverDB.net's own work are under the term above; names and numbers
      read from the WoW Forever client are Blizzard Entertainment's; classic
      data comes from CMaNGOS classic-db (see c).

      How LevelScout meets the term: every data file keeps the "-- Data:"
      line; the attribution appears in this file, in CREDITS.md, in the
      addon's TOC notes and in game under /ls about.

   b. QuestieDB (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), read at runtime
      The database addon of the Questie project, GPL-3.0, a required
      dependency (https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/questiedb). Not
      shipped with LevelScout. LevelScout reads from the installed copy,
      through its LibQuestieDB API and with QuestieDB's own faction and class
      corrections applied: quests and their structure; NPC and object spawns; item drop
      sources; drop rates, which QuestieDB compiled from Wowhead and CMaNGOS
      data; prerequisites, race and class requirements; quest XP; zone mapping
      and race/class masks.

   c. CMaNGOS classic-db (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db)
      The open World of Warcraft Classic 1.12 world database, Copyright (C)
      the CMaNGOS project and its contributors, GPL-3.0. It reaches
      LevelScout through ForeverDB (classic objective counts,
      prerequisites and spawns; shipped) and through QuestieDB (read at
      runtime).
